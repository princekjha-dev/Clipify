# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Clipify community will experience an environment free of harassment. Our community is built on principles of respect, inclusivity, and collaboration.

## Our Standards

To maintain this welcoming environment, all members of the Clipify community are expected to:

### 1. Be Respectful

- Treat all members with respect and courtesy
- Value diverse perspectives and experiences
- Listen actively and with an open mind
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully

### 2. Be Inclusive

- Welcome and encourage participation from all backgrounds
- Be mindful of language that may exclude or alienate others
- Actively work to include underrepresented voices
- Recognize that we all have different experiences and perspectives

### 3. Be Collaborative

- Work together toward common goals
- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Give credit where credit is due
- Support fellow contributors

### 4. Be Professional

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Keep discussions focused and relevant
- Critique ideas, not people
- Be patient and understanding with others' learning curves

### 5. Be Honest

- Communicate openly and truthfully
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them
- Be transparent in your intentions and actions
- Give honest feedback constructively

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated in the Clipify community:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, color, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or disability
- Sexual harassment, unwelcome sexual advances, or requests for sexual favors
- Offensive comments related to any protected characteristic
- Deliberate intimidation or stalking
- Sustained disruptive behavior
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors
- Recording or sharing someone's content without consent
- Sharing personal information (doxing) without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Examples of Harassment Include:

- Using derogatory or demeaning language
- Making unwanted romantic or sexual advances
- Engaging in sustained personal attacks
- Making assumptions about someone's identity or background
- Insulting or name-calling
- Creating a hostile environment through coordinated attacks
- Unwelcome comments about appearance, body, or identity

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the Clipify community, including:

- GitHub repositories, pull requests, and issues
- GitHub Discussions and project messaging
- Community chat channels
- Project wikis and documentation
- Conference talks and presentations
- Unofficial social media spaces used to discuss the project
- Any other community spaces

It also applies to individuals representing the project in public spaces. Examples of representing the project include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as a designated representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **Direct Message**: Contact the project maintainer privately
2. **Email**: Send a detailed report to the project maintainer
3. **GitHub**: Use the "Report abuse" feature on GitHub
4. **Discussion**: Start a private discussion with maintainers

When reporting, please provide:
- Your contact information
- Names of people involved
- Description of what happened
- When and where it occurred
- Any relevant context or documentation

### Confidentiality

We will keep your report confidential and only share information as needed to resolve the issue. We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of all reporters.

### Response Process

When a report is received:

1. We will acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
2. We will investigate the report thoroughly
3. We will communicate our findings to all involved parties
4. We will take appropriate action based on our findings

### Consequences

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in:

- A warning or private conversation about the behavior
- A request to modify or remove a comment
- Temporary removal from community spaces
- Permanent expulsion from the project and community
- Other measures deemed appropriate by project maintainers

The enforcement action will be proportionate to the severity of the violation and the history of the person involved.

## Specific Guidelines

### In Project Discussions

- Stay focused on the technical topic
- Provide evidence for claims
- Be open to correction
- Respect others' time and attention
- Use threads to organize discussions

### In Pull Requests and Issues

- Be specific and constructive in feedback
- Avoid absolute language ("always", "never", "should")
- Suggest improvements rather than criticizing
- Acknowledge effort and progress
- Remember there's a person behind every PR/issue

### In Code Reviews

- Focus on code, not the coder
- Ask questions rather than making demands
- Acknowledge good work
- Be patient with learning curves
- Provide context for suggestions

### In Disagreements

- Assume good intent
- Listen to understand, not to respond
- Look for common ground
- Be willing to compromise
- Disagree respectfully

## Escalation

If you feel your concerns aren't being addressed, you can:

1. Contact a different maintainer
2. Escalate to a senior maintainer or lead
3. Seek mediation from a neutral third party
4. Request a formal review of the situation

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers agree to:

- Enforce this Code of Conduct consistently
- Address violations promptly and fairly
- Maintain confidentiality of reports
- Model the behavior outlined in this Code of Conduct
- Disqualify themselves if personally involved in a report
- Seek input from the community when appropriate
- Document enforcement actions
